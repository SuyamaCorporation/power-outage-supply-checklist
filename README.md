# Power Outage Emergency Supply Checklist

A guided preparedness checklist organized into five categories: Water, Food & Cooking, Medical, Lighting, and Communication. Enter your household size and expected outage duration to auto-calculate recommended quantities for each item, then mark supplies as "Stocked" or "Needed" to track your readiness.

All progress is saved to localStorage, a date-based refresh reminder prompts you to re-check supplies every 90–180 days, and a progress bar shows your overall preparedness percentage at a glance.
